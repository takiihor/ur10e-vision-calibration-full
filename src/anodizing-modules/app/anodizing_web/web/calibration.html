Setup

Use a fixed calibration board. Confirm the camera and active TCP.

Basler requires its own serial and calibrated 12 mm lens intrinsics. Do not use D435 intrinsics. Adjust exposure and gain for the current lighting before capturing; the image must be sharp and evenly exposed.

Camera access requires opt-in. The calibration preview requires 10 corners. Preview camera checks focus, exposure, gain and marker visibility without intrinsics; it does not produce a pose or qualify a calibration.
